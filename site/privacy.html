Privacy Policy

Florida Country Sounds Content Pipeline · Effective October 27, 2026

What this application is

The Florida Country Sounds Content Pipeline ("the App") is a private, self-hosted command-line tool. Its only user is the owner of the Florida Country Sounds channel, who uses it to upload their own audio and video content to their own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Bluesky and Substack. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign in to it.

Information the App accesses

When the owner authorizes the App with a platform, the App receives an access token for the owner's own account. It uses that token only to:

  • upload videos and post captions, titles, descriptions and hashtags written by the owner;
  • read back the ID and status of posts it has created, to confirm they were published.

The App does not read, collect or store data about viewers, followers, subscribers, comments, messages or any other user.

How information is stored

Access tokens and a log of the App's own posts (titles, schedule times, platform post IDs and error messages) are stored only on the owner's personal computer, in a local configuration file and a local SQLite database. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the App, because none exists.

Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share information with anyone. Data is sent only to the platform being posted to, as part of making the post.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Use of Google data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page.

Other platforms

Content posted through the App is also governed by the privacy policies of Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, Bluesky and Substack. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's account settings, which disables the App for that platform.

Data deletion

Revoking access on a platform stops all further use of that account. Deleting the local database and configuration file removes all data the App holds. To request deletion or ask a question, contact CONTACT_EMAIL.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
